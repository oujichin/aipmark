# P-Mark Audit — Common Findings Checklist

## Registry (個人情報管理台帳)

- [ ] All departments' business processes enumerated
- [ ] Web-acquired info included (cookies, IP, access logs)
- [ ] Job applicant personal info included
- [ ] Former employee data handling documented
- [ ] Business card info included
- [ ] Security camera footage identified (if applicable)
- [ ] Personal info received from subcontractors included
- [ ] All purposes of use are specific (not "for business operations")
- [ ] Privacy policy purposes match registry purposes
- [ ] Sensitive personal info (要配慮個人情報) flagged
- [ ] My Number (マイナンバー) managed separately

## Risk Analysis

- [ ] Every registry business process has a risk entry
- [ ] Both digital AND paper media risks assessed
- [ ] Subcontractor-originated risks assessed
- [ ] Risk ratings reflect reality (not all "medium")
- [ ] Countermeasures are specific (not "be careful")
- [ ] Residual risk evaluated
- [ ] Human threats assessed (misoperation, insider fraud)
- [ ] Physical threats assessed (theft, loss, disaster)
- [ ] Technical threats assessed (unauthorized access, malware)

## Third-party / Subcontractor

- [ ] Third-party sharing: consent method documented
- [ ] Subcontractor list up to date
- [ ] Cloud services (SaaS) recognized as subcontracting
- [ ] Contracts include data protection clauses
- [ ] Sub-subcontracting managed

## Security Measures

- [ ] Physical: entry control, locks, anti-peeping
- [ ] Technical: access control, encryption, log management
- [ ] Human: training, NDAs, offboarding procedures
- [ ] Organizational: officer appointed, policies maintained, audits conducted

## Consent & Individual Rights

- [ ] Purpose disclosed at acquisition
- [ ] Consent method recorded (form checkbox, etc.)
- [ ] Disclosure request procedure established
- [ ] Complaint handling desk established

## Commonly Overlooked Personal Info

| Category | Examples |
|----------|----------|
| Web | Contact form inputs, cookies, IP, access logs |
| Recruitment | Resumes, work history, interview notes, aptitude tests |
| Employee | Salary, attendance, health checkups, My Number |
| Business partners | Business cards, contact info, credit info |
| Physical | Security camera footage, entry/exit logs, reception records |
| External services | Job site applicants, EC site customers |
