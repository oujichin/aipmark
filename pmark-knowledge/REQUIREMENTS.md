# JIS Q 15001 Key Requirements (P-Mark Audit)

## A.3.3.1 — Identification of Personal Information
Organization must identify all personal info it handles.
- Enumerate per business process: type, data subjects, volume, purpose
- Document as a registry (台帳)

### Common deficiencies
- Vague identification ("info used for internal operations")
- Missing: cookies, IP addresses, access logs from websites
- Missing: job applicants, former employees
- Missing: personal info received from subcontractors
- Missing: security camera footage, business cards

## A.3.3.3 — Risk Recognition, Analysis & Countermeasures
Analyze leakage/loss/damage risks for all identified personal info.
- Evaluate threat + vulnerability pairs
- Calculate risk = likelihood × impact
- Assess whether residual risk is acceptable after current measures

### Common deficiencies
- All risks rated "medium" (doesn't reflect reality)
- Countermeasures like "be careful" or "pay attention" (too vague)
- Only digital risks assessed, paper risks missing
- Subcontractor-originated risks not evaluated

## A.3.4.2.1 — Specification of Purpose of Use
Purpose must be as specific as possible.
- NG: 「事業活動のため」 → OK: 「商品の発送および配送状況のご連絡のため」
- Privacy policy stated purpose must match registry

## A.3.4.2.4 — Measures at Acquisition
When acquiring personal info, inform the individual and obtain consent.
- Written/web form acquisition requires prior disclosure
- Record consent method (checkbox, signature, etc.)

## A.3.4.3.2 — Subcontractor Supervision
When outsourcing personal info handling, exercise necessary supervision.
- Define selection criteria
- Include data protection clauses in contracts
- Periodically verify subcontractor compliance

### Common deficiencies
- Cloud services (SaaS) not recognized as outsourcing
- Subcontractor list not up to date
- Sub-subcontracting not managed

## A.3.4.5 — Education
Conduct personal info protection training for all employees.
- At least once per year
- New hire orientation
- Maintain records (attendees, content, date)

## A.3.7 — Internal Audit
Conduct internal audit at least once per year.
- Auditors must not audit their own department
- Maintain: audit plan, checklist, corrective action records

---

## Mandatory Confirmation Items (must be confirmed, NOT estimated)

These are the backbone of the registry. Agent must obtain confirmed evidence
or generate questions (unconfirmed) — never leave as estimated:

1. **Purpose of use** per business process — basis for consent
2. **Acquisition method** — needed to verify consent mechanism
3. **Third-party sharing** (yes/no + recipients) — requires consent or legal basis
4. **Subcontractors** (yes/no + scope) — triggers supervision obligation
5. **Sensitive personal info** (health, criminal, beliefs, etc.) — requires explicit consent + additional safeguards

## Fields allowed as estimated in drafts
- Retention period (if inferable from industry norms, e.g. tax law 7 years)
- Access scope (department-level estimate OK)
- Disposal method (generic method OK)
- Volume (rough estimate OK)
- Storage location detail ("cloud service" level OK)
